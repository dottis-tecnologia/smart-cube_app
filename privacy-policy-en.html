Privacy Policy - Cube4Meters

Effective date: May 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Cube4Meters collects the following information to provide our services:

  • Authentication Data: Email, name, and user ID
  • Device Data: Device information for compatibility
  • Usage Data: Meter readings, locations, and captured images
  • Technical Data: Error logs and performance information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate users and manage access
  • Store meter readings locally and sync with server
  • Provide technical support and improve service quality
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
  • Ensure app security and prevent fraud

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is protected with:

  • Local Storage: SQLite database on your device
  • Server Storage: Encrypted database in Brazil
  • Authentication: JWT tokens stored securely
  • Transmission: SSL/TLS encryption for all data

4. Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell your personal information
  • Share data with third parties for marketing
  • Transfer data internationally

We only share data when required by law or with your explicit consent.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your data in a readable format
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • User Account: Until you request deletion
  • Reading Data: 2 years (as required by law)
  • Technical Logs: 90 days
  • Deleted Data: Permanently removed within 30 days

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or email.
